My dearest love,

If you've made it all the way here, then I guess you've read all the little things I've prepared for you. And honestly, I hope every letter, every little detail, and every song has made you feel even a fraction of how much you mean to me.

I don't think words will ever be enough to explain how grateful I am to have you in my life. But I still want to try, because you deserve to be reminded of how special you are.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for all the little moments, the random conversations, the silly jokes, the laughter, and even the quiet moments when we don't really have anything to say. Somehow, even the simplest things feel special when they're with you.

I love getting to know you more and more. I love discovering the little things about you that I didn't notice before. I love hearing about your day, listening to your stories, and being someone you can share your thoughts with. Even the smallest updates from you can make an ordinary day feel a little brighter.

And I know we're not perfect. There will be days when things don't go as planned, when we're tired, when we misunderstand each other, or when finding the right words feels a little difficult. But I hope we never forget that behind all those moments, there's still so much love worth taking care of.

I don't need us to have the most perfect love story. I just want something real with you. Something where we can laugh freely, grow together, learn from our mistakes, and feel comfortable being exactly who we are. I want to keep making memories with you, whether they're big adventures or the tiniest, most ordinary moments that we'll probably laugh about years from now.

If I could tell you one thing today, it would be this: I'm really, really glad that it's you. Out of all the people I could have met, I'm grateful that our paths crossed and that I get to call you my love.

I hope life is gentle with you. I hope you get to achieve the things you've been dreaming of. I hope you never lose the little spark that makes you who you are. And whenever life gets a little overwhelming, I hope you remember that you don't have to figure everything out all at once.

Dari sekian banyaknya manusia, terima kasih sudah memilih aku, sayang. Lalu, terima kasih juga sudah ketuk DM-ku di hari itu. Siapa sangka, dari pesan random itu, sebagian dari hidupku berubah dalam sekejap? Banyak-banyak terima kasih aku ucapkan karna kamu sudah susah payah memberi warna pada seseorang yang selama ini mengira hidupnya hanya akan terus dibalut dengan hitam dan putih. Sayangku, tolong ingat; Kamu itu cukup, dan akan selalu cukup.

Thank you for being part of my days, my thoughts, and so many of my favorite memories.

Happy anniversary, sayang.

I love you now, tomorrow, and forever.

With all my love,

Your favorite person, Al.
